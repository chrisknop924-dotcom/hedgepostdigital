Armor Ink DTF Transfers Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: The Armor Ink DTF Transfers app is the Armor Ink store (armorink.com) on your phone. When you order, sign in or upload artwork, the store collects what it needs to fill and ship your order. The store's pages inside the app also run advertising and analytics tags from companies such as Meta, Google, TikTok and Pinterest, which receive what you view and buy. Photos you try on a shirt in the app stay on your phone.

Who we are

The Armor Ink DTF Transfers app ("the app") is published on Google Play by Hedgepost Digital LLC, on behalf of Armor Ink LLC. Armor Ink LLC runs the store at armorink.com and is the seller for every order placed in the app. Both companies have the same owner and are at 14 N Main St, Ellinwood, Kansas 67526, USA.

For questions about your orders, your account or your information, contact Armor Ink at support@armorink.com or 620-900-1322. For questions about the app itself, contact Hedgepost Digital at contact@hedgepostdigital.com.

Information that stays on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only. It is not sent to Armor Ink, Hedgepost Digital or anyone else:

  • Items in your cart before you check out
  • Your settings, such as the state you picked for delivery estimates
  • Photos you take or choose for the shirt preview. They are used on your phone to show a design on your shirt and are never uploaded.

Deleting the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes all of it.

Information collected when you shop

Signing in, checking out and uploading artwork happen on the store's own pages, shown inside the app. Through them, Armor Ink collects:

  • Contact and account details: your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), shipping and billing addresses, and your account ID. Used to take, ship and support your orders, to run your account (you sign in with your email and a one-time code), and to send order emails. Marketing emails are sent only if you sign up for them, and every one has an unsubscribe link.
  • Payment details: card details you enter at checkout go to Shopify's secure checkout and its payment processors. Armor Ink and Hedgepost Digital never see your full card number. Used to take payment and to prevent fraud.
  • Purchase history: what you ordered and paid. Used to fill orders, keep business records and understand what sells.
  • Artwork and files you upload for gang sheets and transfers by size. Used only to print your order.
  • Activity in the store: pages and products you view, items you add to your cart, and what you search for.
  • Device and online identifiers, and approximate location: cookies and similar identifiers in the app's web pages, and your general area (such as your city or state) worked out from your internet address. This app does not use your phone's GPS or advertising ID.

Who receives it

  • Companies that run the store for Armor Ink: Shopify (the store and checkout), payment processors, shipping carriers such as UPS, and the apps that power parts of the store, such as the gang sheet builder and product reviews. They use your information only to provide those services.
  • Advertising and analytics partners: the store's pages, including inside the app, run tags from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google, TikTok, Pinterest and similar services. They receive the pages and products you view, cart and purchase events with the order value, cookie identifiers, and your approximate location. To match purchases to ads, they may also receive your email address or phone number in a scrambled (hashed) form. Armor Ink uses this to measure and improve its advertising, and these companies may use it to show you ads. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect against fraud.

Your choices

  • You can browse without an account. You need to give contact and payment details only to place an order.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.
  • You can limit personalized ads in your Google and Meta account settings, and clearing the app's data resets the cookies it holds.
  • You can ask Armor Ink to see, correct or delete your information by emailing support@armorink.com.

Keeping it safe

Everything the app and the store's pages send travels encrypted (HTTPS). Payments are handled by Shopify's checkout, which meets the card industry's security standard (PCI DSS).

How long it is kept

Account details are kept while you have an account. Order records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Advertising and analytics partners keep what they receive under their own policies.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and its data, follow the steps at armorink.com/pages/delete-account, or email support@armorink.com from the address on your account. Armor Ink deletes it within 30 days, except order records it must keep for tax purposes.

Children

The app is for adults who buy printing supplies and transfers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The store's own policy

Armor Ink's privacy policy for armorink.com, at armorink.com/policies/privacy-policy, gives more detail about the store and applies to orders placed in the app too.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
