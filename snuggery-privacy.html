Snuggery Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version: Snuggery plays audiobooks you already own, from files on your phone. Your books, listening progress, bookmarks, and notes stay on your device. If you turn on Google Drive backup, Snuggery saves a copy to your own Google Drive, which we never see. There are no ads, no accounts with us, and no tracking.

Who we are

Snuggery is published by Hedgepost Digital LLC, 14 N Main St, Ellinwood, Kansas 67526, USA. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@hedgepostdigital.com.

Information that stays on your phone

To play your audiobooks and remember where you left off, Snuggery keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your library: the audiobook folders you choose, plus titles, authors, chapters, and cover art read from those files
  • Your listening position, bookmarks, notes, and listening history
  • Your settings, such as playback speed and skip lengths

None of this is sent to Hedgepost Digital or any server we run. Deleting the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it from your phone.

Your audiobook files

Snuggery only opens the folders you pick. It reads those files to play them and never uploads, shares, or changes them.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off until you turn it on. When you do, you sign in with your Google account and Snuggery saves your library information, listening positions, bookmarks, and notes to your Google Drive so you can restore them on a new phone. If you also choose to back up your audiobook files, those are copied to your Drive too.

  • Snuggery can only access the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot see or open anything else stored there.
  • Your backup is stored in your own Google account. Hedgepost Digital never receives a copy and cannot access it.
  • You can turn backup off at any time in Snuggery's settings, and delete the backup from your Google Drive.

Snuggery's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions Snuggery asks for

  • Files and media: used only to read the audiobook folders you choose.
  • Notifications: used to show playback controls while a book is playing.
  • Network access: used only for Google Drive backup and restore, when you turn it on.

Purchases

Premium features are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment entirely. We never see your payment details. Google tells the app whether your purchase is active so premium features can be turned on.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't require an account with us or ask for your name or email.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information.

Children

Snuggery is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
